Overview & Context
"An active community is
an effective community."

Given to us as a fact. And it is.

So, what does design have to do with it?

People need a reason to belong, to stay, to come back.

That's when it hit me. It's not an accident, but rather a Design Decision.

That's how I found ATFI, and exactly the problem they had.

The Problem
A decade of impact.
An online presence stuck in 2012.

Since 2015: four regional branches, free training, mentorship. On paper, everything was there.

What the site was
A passive brochure, no way to join
A dated look
Nothing for young engineers
What ATFI had
A 10-year mission
Influential members and mentors
National reach, 4 cities
Central question
How do you make an existing community feel worth joining for the next generation?

Ideation & Direction
The design process wasn't linear.
It pivoted. Twice.
Testing & Iteration
The pivot wasn't a failure.
It was the process working.

AttrakDiff and NPS on the first version: more appealing, still too institutional. So I rebuilt the site map around community and action, and gave ATFI Jeunes its own space.

What we added
Four additions,
each from the research.
01

Impact, front and centre

Numbers, partners and photos right under the hero.

Why it mattersProof in the first scroll shows a newcomer this community is real.

02

An events calendar on the homepage

Upcoming events by city, one tap to join.

Why it mattersA reason for veterans to return, an easy first step for juniors.

03

Testimonials from members

Real quotes from members, students to project leads.

Why it mattersJuniors need women to look up to. A peer’s voice beats intimidation.

04

A mobile menu built for thumbs

Six clear entries, sign-up always visible.

Why it mattersJuniors live on their phones; joining is never more than a tap away.
